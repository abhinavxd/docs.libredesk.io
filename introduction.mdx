Libredesk is a free and open-source omnichannel customer support desk with built-in live chat, email, and more. The backend is written in Go and the frontend in Vue JS with ShadcN for UI components.

  className="rounded-lg border"
/>

Visit [libredesk.io](https://libredesk.io) for more info. Check out the [**live demo**](https://demo.libredesk.io/).

## Features

- **Omnichannel inbox**  
  Live chat and email conversations arrive in the same inbox.

- **Live chat widget**  
  Embed a chat widget on your website with a single script tag. Visitors chat from any page, and agents reply from the same inbox.

- **Granular permissions**  
  Create custom roles with granular permissions for teams and individual agents.

- **Automations**  
  Auto-tag, assign, and route conversations with rules that run on conditions you define.

- **CSAT surveys**  
  Measure customer satisfaction with automated surveys.

- **Macros**  
  Save the replies you send often, along with the tags and actions that go with them, and apply them in one click.

- **Organization**  
  Tags, custom conversation statuses, snoozing, and search across every conversation.

- **Auto assignment**  
  Assign incoming conversations by agent capacity or your own criteria.

- **SLA management**  
  Set response and resolution targets, and get notified before a conversation breaches one.

- **Custom attributes**  
  Create custom attributes for contacts or conversations such as the subscription plan or the date of their first purchase.

- **AI-assist**  
  Rewrite replies with AI to adjust tone or fix grammar.

- **Activity logs**  
  An audit log of what agents and admins changed, and when.
